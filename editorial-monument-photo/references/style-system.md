# Style system

## Visual DNA

This family combines five systems:

1. **Documentary subject** — realistic landmark, street, interior, object, or person with credible light and material detail.
2. **Monumental type** — one enormous condensed word, often occupying 45-70% of the canvas.
3. **Layered depth** — type behind the subject; selected edges may disappear behind roofs, heads, shoulders, trees, or foreground objects.
4. **Editorial restraint** — warm paper, muted red, charcoal, stone, low saturation, sparse annotation.
5. **Archival tactility** — fine grain, slightly uneven ink density, soft highlight roll-off, never a damaged-vintage gimmick.

## Composition ranges

| Property | Target |
|---|---|
| Hero word | 45-70% of canvas height; ultra-condensed; tight tracking |
| Type side safe zone | target 5-8% each side; hard minimum 4%; never crop letters |
| Main subject | 52-62% of canvas height for city landmarks; 45-65% tolerance only |
| Negative space | 15-28%; 30% is the upper limit before the poster feels hollow |
| Microcopy | 2-5 compact blocks maximum; optional |
| Horizon | lower third for a place; mid/lower third for a person |
| Camera | frontal, axial, or restrained three-quarter view |
| Foreground | 0-15% visual complexity; simple water, steps, pavement, or low planting only |

## Palette

- warm paper: `#F2EBDD` to `#E9DFCF`
- brick/cinnabar: `#B74432` to `#C94B39`
- charcoal: `#252521`
- stone: `#8E8478`
- optional sage: `#7F887B`

Avoid pure white, neon red, saturated cyan, glossy black, and broad rainbow palettes.

## Lighting and texture

- soft overcast, hazy morning, or late-afternoon directional daylight
- controlled contrast; retained shadow detail
- natural materials and fine surface detail
- subtle offset-lithograph or matte art-book texture
- restrained halation only around strong highlights

## Typography behavior

Describe the hero type as: `monumental ultra-condensed grotesk uppercase lettering, very tall vertical proportions, tight tracking, flat muted brick-red ink, integrated behind the subject`.

Safe-distance behavior:

- keep every letter inside the canvas with 5-8% side breathing room
- never let first or last letters touch, clip, or visually lean against the edge
- for long city names, compress the type and reduce tracking before reducing the landmark too far
- if type becomes too small or the poster feels empty, use a two-row lockup only when the user allows it; otherwise choose a medium-close landmark scale and tighter type

For Chinese hero type, use: `2-4 monumental Chinese characters in a tall geometric display style`, but expect lower text reliability and plan deterministic overlay.

## Place-specific controls

- preserve known silhouette, facade rhythm, materials, and scale
- make the landmark the single dominant visual anchor
- prefer a closer architectural portrait when the user names a landmark
- keep city landmarks in a medium-close scale: large enough to command attention, small enough to preserve type margins
- avoid fantasy restoration or duplicated towers/windows
- include only a few contextual elements: one calm water/river strip, simple steps, quiet pavement, a few trees, or low distant skyline
- keep vehicles and crowds secondary
- avoid busy foregrounds, dense trees, many boats, crowded bridges, cluttered street furniture, heavy traffic, and competing buildings
- reject compositions that solve clutter by pulling the subject so far back that the poster becomes mostly empty paper

## Person-specific controls

- real adult; specify age range and role when known
- natural skin pores, realistic hair, correct fingers, relaxed shoulders
- thoughtful neutral expression; no exaggerated smile or influencer pose
- wardrobe: linen, wool, cotton, matte leather; quiet solids
- environmental context must remain legible
- hero word behind body; never across eyes or mouth

## Always avoid

`generic travel advertisement, souvenir poster, glossy commercial campaign, luxury fashion ad, cyberpunk, orange-and-teal cinema grade, HDR, neon, red-yellow shock poster, sticker collage, scrapbook, fake Polaroid border, torn paper, heavy scratches, extreme sepia, grunge overload, random logos, seals, flags, invented coordinates, filler paragraphs, gibberish typography, multiple competing headlines, pasted-on text box, 3D chrome lettering, duplicated buildings, busy foreground, dense trees blocking the landmark, many boats, crowded bridge, heavy traffic, cluttered street furniture, competing landmarks, warped perspective, plastic skin, deformed face, extra fingers, malformed hands, floating objects`
